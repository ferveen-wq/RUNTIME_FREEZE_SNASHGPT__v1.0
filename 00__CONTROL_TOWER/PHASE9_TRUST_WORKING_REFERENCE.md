
# PHASE 9 — TRUST & CREDIBILITY (WORKING REFERENCE)

STATUS:
Pre-rollout consolidation layer (non-runtime)

---

## PURPOSE

Phase 9 exists to reinforce confidence after education and visual understanding.

It is NOT for:
- introducing new information
- pushing decisions aggressively

It is for:
- reducing doubt
- strengthening trust
- confirming correctness of decision

---

## CORE TRUST LAYERS

### 1. INSTALLATION CREDIBILITY

Concept:
Installation quality determines long-term result more than brand alone.

Anchors:
- preparation quality
- edge finishing
- alignment precision

---

### 2. VISUAL CONFIRMATION

Concept:
Customers trust what they can see on real vehicles.

Supports:
- result showcase
- similar vehicle examples
- before/after clarity

---

### 3. REAL CUSTOMER PROOF

Concept:
Other customer experiences reduce hesitation.

Supports:
- testimonials
- real feedback
- delivered results

---

### 4. BRAND & PROCESS TRUST

Concept:
Confidence in workshop, tools, and standards.

Supports:
- shop environment
- team expertise
- installation process transparency

---

### 5. LONG-TERM REALITY

Concept:
Durability matters more than first-day appearance.

Supports:
- GCC conditions
- aging behavior
- maintenance expectations

---

### 6. HONEST LIMITATIONS

Concept:
Transparency increases trust.

Supports:
- what PPF does NOT do
- what ceramic does NOT do
- realistic expectations
