# Bilingual Phrase Integrity Guidelines

These guidelines are designed to ensure the integrity and quality of bilingual phrases, specifically between English (EN) and Arabic (AR). Adhering to these rules will help maintain consistency, accuracy, and cultural relevance in bilingual content.

## 1. General Translation Accuracy

- Ensure that the Arabic translation accurately reflects the meaning of the English source.
- Avoid literal translations that do not convey the intended message.

## 2. Cultural Relevance

- Adapt phrases to be culturally relevant and appropriate for the Arabic-speaking audience.
- Avoid references, idioms, or expressions that may not be understood or appreciated in the Arab culture.

## 3. Linguistic Quality

- Maintain high linguistic quality in both languages, with correct grammar, syntax, and punctuation.
- Ensure that the Arabic phrases are written in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) unless a specific dialect is required.

## 4. Consistency

- Use consistent terminology and phrasing in both languages throughout the document.
- Refer to approved glossaries or term bases for specific terms or phrases.

## 5. Structural Symmetry Rule

If the English phrase structure is modified (for example splitting one sentence
into multiple lines, adding questions, or restructuring the phrasing), the
Arabic phrase must be updated to mirror the same structure.

Allowed:

EN:
Sentence 1.
Sentence 2.

AR:
جملة 1.
جملة 2.

Not allowed:

EN:
Sentence 1.
Sentence 2.

AR:
جملة واحدة طويلة تمثل النسخة القديمة.

This prevents silent bilingual drift during phrase refinement.

## 6. Review and Approval

- All bilingual phrases should be reviewed and approved by qualified linguists or subject matter experts.
- Implement a quality assurance process to verify the accuracy and quality of the translations.

## 7. Continuous Improvement

- Encourage feedback and suggestions for improving bilingual phrases.
- Regularly update and refine phrases to enhance clarity, accuracy, and cultural relevance.
